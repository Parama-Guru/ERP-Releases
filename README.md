## Updates

Installed copies check for new versions by themselves. When one has downloaded, an **Update** button appears at the
top right of the window; click it to restart into the new version. The portable Windows app, and the Mac app until it
is signed, do not update themselves: download the new version from this page.

Your books stay on your computer and are never changed by an update. Keep copies with **Settings › Backups**.

## First start

The installers are not code-signed yet, so the operating system asks once before the first start:

- **Windows:** at "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info › Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** if macOS says it cannot check the app, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click
  **Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 and earlier, right-click the app and choose **Open** also works).
